PulseDodge Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how 1Dev Games handles information when you play PulseDodge.

At a glance

PulseDodge has no player account, sign-in, or developer-operated cloud save. Game progress is stored on your device. The game uses Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded ads and Google Play Billing for premium cosmetic purchases. The ad request sets Google's maximum ad content rating to G (general audiences). We do not operate an analytics or advertising server of our own.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score, coin balance, daily bonus date, owned and equipped skins, and game settings in the app's local storage. This information is used to maintain your progress and preferences. The game does not upload this save file to 1Dev Games.

Local save data remains on the device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. Device backup behavior depends on Android and your device settings; we do not promise that local saves will be backed up or restored. Premium purchase ownership is separately queried from Google Play when available.

Google Mobile Ads

PulseDodge uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load optional rewarded ads, such as an ad that may offer a revive. The game configures the SDK's maximum ad content rating as G (general audiences). This is an ad-content rating setting; it does not by itself guarantee that every ad is suitable for every age or determine whether the app or SDK meets requirements that apply to apps targeting children. Google may collect or receive information from the app and device to provide, measure, secure, and improve its ad services. Depending on the SDK, device, region, and applicable choices, this may include IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics or fraud-prevention information.

The current development configuration uses Google's official rewarded test ad unit; production ad configuration may differ. Ad availability, data use, and consent requirements are subject to Google's policies, the player's location and settings, and applicable law.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google Play Billing

Premium cosmetic purchases are processed by Google Play. The game receives purchase details such as product identifiers, purchase state, package information, and a purchase token so it can check the transaction with Google Play, acknowledge eligible purchases, and restore purchased skins. The game does not receive or store your payment-card number or other payment credentials. 1Dev Games does not send purchase tokens to a developer-operated server.

Google Play handles payment information under its own terms and privacy disclosures. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Payments Privacy Notice.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. The game has no developer-operated backend and does not send local scores, coins, settings, or skin selections to 1Dev Games. Information handled by Google Mobile Ads and Google Play is shared directly with Google as needed for those services and is subject to Google's policies. Google may process information in countries other than the one where you live.

No other analytics, crash-reporting, sign-in, or cloud-save service is currently integrated into PulseDodge.

Retention and choices

You can remove locally stored game progress by clearing PulseDodge's app storage or uninstalling the game. Google retains information it processes according to its own policies and controls. Uninstalling PulseDodge does not remove Google Play purchase history; eligible purchases may be restored through Google Play when you use the same Google account.

You can review Google's privacy and ad controls through your Google account and Android settings. Availability and effect of controls depend on your location, device, and account.

Children

PulseDodge does not have a player account or profile. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information from the app and device as described above. Setting the maximum ad content rating to G does not change the SDK's data practices or replace any child-directed settings, consent measures, SDK certifications, or other steps that may be required based on the app's declared audience and applicable policies or laws. Parents or guardians with privacy questions can contact us using the details below.

Security

Local game data is kept in the app's private storage area. Information transmitted by Google's services is handled under Google's security practices. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected here.
